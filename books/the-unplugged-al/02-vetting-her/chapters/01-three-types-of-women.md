# The Three Types of Women (And How You Know Which One She Is)

Most men sort women by attraction. Useless for the long game.

Sort by behavior pattern — how she treats you over time, not how she makes you feel on date three. There are three types. One usually dominates.

## Type One: The Partner

She wants both of you winning. She has her own life, her own direction, and doesn't need you to shrink so she can feel tall.

**How you know:**
- Disagrees without demeaning.
- Handles disappointment without a punishment campaign.
- Takes responsibility when she's wrong.
- Celebrates your progress instead of competing with it.
- Protects your reputation when you're not in the room.
- Repairs after conflict instead of scorekeeping.

Under stress, she pushes back harder than usual. She still comes back to center. That return to center is the marker.

## Type Two: The Consumer

She values what you provide more than who you are without the provision — attention, money, status, emotional labor. Early she seems warm. The invoice grows.

**How you know:**
- Her affection tracks closely with what you give.
- Entitlement grows as investment grows.
- Your problems are interruptions. Hers are emergencies.
- "Fair" always seems to mean you give more.
- Competes with your mission instead of integrating with it.

Under stress, the gap between what she takes and what she gives widens.

She's not always malicious. Some were raised to see male value as utility. That doesn't make her the right choice — and it doesn't make you her rehabilitation project.

## Type Three: The Chaos Agent

She runs on intensity. High highs, low lows, constant drama. Magnetic. Also exhausting. Your calm becomes the enemy.

**How you know:**
- Frequent crises requiring you to drop everything.
- Triangulation — exes, jealousy, other men used as leverage.
- Rules for you that don't apply to her.
- Conflict turns destructive: insults, threats, public scenes.
- Apologies without changed behavior.

What this feels like: intense, passionate, alive. That's your nervous system on adrenaline. The pull is real. So is the cost.

## How to Sort

You're not labeling her forever. You're reading current patterns to set your investment level.

Partners: worth patience through normal friction.
Consumers: need firm, early limits.
Chaos Agents: need exit plans — often immediate.

Believe what repeats across different contexts: work stress, money conversations, family events. Not what she promises in one sincere moment at midnight.
