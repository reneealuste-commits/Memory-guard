# The Unplugged Man's Code

Ten principles. Not a summary of what you read — a distillation of who you are becoming. Carry these when you are tired, winning, rebuilding, or tempted back into old patterns.

---

1. **You are the project.** Everything else is built on what you build in yourself first. Neglect the foundation and nothing above it holds for long.

2. **Peace is something you build, not something you earn.** A life with chronic chaos is not a love story — it is a failure of standards. Protect your baseline like you protect your health.

3. **Standards are kindness with a spine.** A clear, early no saves both of you from a slow disaster. The men who hurt women most are the ones who said yes for years and then collapsed — not the ones who said no calmly at the start.

4. **Vet slow. Give time to character.** Chemistry arrives in a day. Character takes months to reveal. Do not let the speed of attraction determine the depth of your commitment.

5. **A full life is both your protection and your gift.** The man with purpose, honest brothers, and solid structure is hard to collapse — and worth choosing. Build it before you invite anyone into it.

6. **The small moments are the real ones.** How you hold a limit. How she responds to one. These quiet exchanges contain more truth than any grand romantic gesture ever will.

7. **Walk before you become someone you do not recognize.** Long-term wrong situations do not just drain resources — they reshape identity. Protect who you are becoming as fiercely as what you have built.

8. **Recovery is construction, not processing.** Floor first. Walls second. Roof last. You do not decorate before you have structure. Rebuild behavior before you rebuild trust in anyone new.

9. **Be strong where it counts. Not hard where it hurts.** Strength serves the people around you. Hardness protects your wound. Learn the difference. Build one. Set the other down.

10. **Your life is the message.** Not the things you say, not the principles you post, not the man you plan to become. The life you actually build and the way you live it — that is what remains. Build something worth reading.

---

## Audit One Rule This Week

The principle that hit hardest while reading — that is the one for this week.

**Weekly practice:**
1. Write one way this week you lived by this code. One way you broke it. Be specific.
2. Name the principle each connects to.
3. Choose one action this week that represents the code in practice — not the belief, the action.
4. Tell one person you trust what you are working on.
5. At week's end: did your life get slightly more solid, or slightly less? Honest answer only.

One principle per month. Let the year reshape you by repetition, not by drama.

When you slip — return without theater. One honest action. One kept promise. One real conversation with a man you trust.

You are not unplugging to hate the world. You are unplugging to stop bleeding in the same loops and start building something that holds.

You are already becoming him.
