# The Unplugged Man — Cover Brand Guide

Premium KDP ebook series. Six books. One visual identity.

## Series Positioning

**Feel:** Cinematic, mature, editorial — not gym-bro, not red-pill meme, not stock-photo romance.

**Reference quality bar:** Ryan Holiday hardcovers, Mark Manson premium editions, *Atomic Habits* hardcover simplicity, high-end business memoir aesthetics.

**Audience signal:** A man in his 30s–50s who has been through real pain and wants clarity — not hype.

---

## Technical Specs (KDP / Grok Imagine)

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Ebook cover ratio | **2:3** (1600 × 2560 px recommended) |
| Safe text zone | Top **40%** of cover — keep imagery below or very subtle behind text |
| Title safe margins | 80 px from all edges |
| Export | PNG or JPG, RGB, 300 DPI equivalent |

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Charcoal | `#1a1a1f` | Primary background |
| Deep navy | `#0d1b2a` | Secondary background, gradients |
| Warm bronze | `#c9a227` | Accent lines, series branding |
| Antique gold | `#8b6914` | Subtle highlights |
| Off-white | `#f5f0e8` | Title typography |
| Smoke | `#6b6b73` | Subtitle, author line |

**Gradient formula:** charcoal → deep navy, top to bottom. Bronze accent as thin horizontal rule or corner detail.

## Typography (add in Canva/Figma after Grok generates art)

| Element | Font direction | Style |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Series label | Small caps sans | `THE UNPLUGGED MAN` — bronze, letter-spaced |
| Book number | Sans, light | `BOOK 1` — smoke gray |
| Title | Bold serif | Large, off-white, high contrast |
| Subtitle | Sans, regular | 2 lines max, smoke gray |
| Author | Sans, small caps | `THE UNPLUGGED AL` — bronze |

**Recommended free fonts:** Playfair Display (title), Inter or Montserrat (subtitle/author).

---

## Visual Rules (all 6 covers)

1. **No faces** — silhouettes, hands, or symbolic objects only. Avoid identifiable people.
2. **No women on covers** — keeps focus on the man's journey, avoids dating-app aesthetic.
3. **One strong symbolic object** per cover — not cluttered.
4. **Moody lighting** — single light source, cinematic shadows.
5. **Texture** — subtle film grain or paper texture for premium feel.
6. **Consistent layout** — series name top, title center, author bottom third.
