# The Unplugged Al

**Straight talk for men who are tired of bleeding in relationships and ready to lead their own life.**

## Core Promise

This series teaches you how to stop repeating the same painful patterns with women, how to carry yourself with real strength, and how to build a life that doesn't fall apart every time a relationship ends or gets difficult.

## Target Reader

Men who have been through painful relationships, divorces, or years of feeling like they keep choosing the wrong women. Men who are ready to stop simping, stop over-investing, and start leading their own life with clarity and self-respect.

## Series Structure

Six short, powerful books (each 120–160 pages). Each book stands alone but builds on the one before it.

| 1 | Frame First | How to hold your ground when she tests you, gets emotional, ... |
| 2 | Vetting Her | How to see who she really is before you give her your time, ... |
| 3 | Stop Simping | Why you keep over-investing, pedestalizing, and choosing the... |
| 4 | Male Space | Why you need other men and a mission bigger than any woman. |
| 5 | Money & Women | How to protect your resources without becoming stingy or par... |
| 6 | The Unplugged Man's Code | The final principles for self-respect, strong relationships,... |
