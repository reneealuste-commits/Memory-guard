# The Stop Simping Man's Code

Not a performance for women. A contract with yourself. Read it when you're about to over-invest, explain away disrespect, or send the text you already know you shouldn't.

## The 10 Rules

**1. Her approval is feedback. Not oxygen.**
Want it. Don't require it to function. A man who needs her validation to feel okay is a man she can't respect long-term.

**2. Match effort. Never fund a one-sided arrangement.**
Warm starts are fine. Sustained one-way labor is not. If you keep raising the bid and she keeps calling, stop playing that hand.

**3. Name it before you do it.**
Before you apologize to end tension (not because you were wrong), before you cancel your plans for her mood, before you send the third unanswered text — say it plainly: *this is simping.* Seen is slowed.

**4. Her past explains her. It doesn't excuse ongoing disrespect of you.**
Compassion is real. Unlimited tolerance is self-erasure with a kinder label.

**5. Stop apologizing for having standards.**
Wanting consistency and reciprocity is not aggression. Apologizing for it teaches people it's negotiable.

**6. Never beg — not in words, not in gifts, not in presence.**
State your position once, maybe twice. After that you're not clarifying. You're bidding your dignity down.

**7. Full availability isn't devotion. It's fear.**
You can love her and still have a schedule, sleep, and your own priorities. Constant access signals you have nothing else going on.

**8. Use the brother test.**
Would you advise your closest friend to do what you're about to do? Simping survives in isolation. Hold it up to honesty and it shrinks.

**9. Walk when the pattern is clear.**
Time doesn't fix a dynamic built on your over-functioning. Walking sooner saves years and self-respect both.

**10. Self-respect is built in private decisions nobody applauds.**
Workout. Work. Brotherhood. Honesty. No one claps for it. Do it anyway, every day.

## How to Use It

Memorize the two or three rules that sting most. Those are yours.

Read this before you:
- Send a long explanation after she went cold
- Buy something to reset her interest
- Cancel the gym because she's upset
- Stay in something you already know is one-sided

## Audit One Rule This Week

The rule that stings most when you read it — that is the one you are living inside right now.

**Weekly practice:**
1. Name one specific behavior this week that was simping.
2. Identify which of the ten rules it violated.
3. Write what you will do differently the next time that moment arrives.
4. Tell one man you trust what you are working on.
5. Check in with him next week. Accountability outlasts willpower.

Break a rule. Return the same day. The code doesn't ask for perfection. It asks you to stop the slow bleed of a man who gives away what he should protect.

Not to win her. To win yourself back.
